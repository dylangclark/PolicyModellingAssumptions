Interpretation

Observed values, published forecasts, policy targets, model parameters and sensitivities are distinct record types. Cross-source ranges will be labelled as planning envelopes unless they are calibrated using historical forecast errors. They are not automatically statistical confidence intervals.

All records retain source, period, vintage and document provenance. Source-specific licences control public redistribution.
